Improving the FY2025 budget and forecast process

53of 124 line items where the reforecast beat the budget
The reforecast adds no accuracy over the original budget. Three diagnostics, three fixes.

1. Forecast bias by department

+3.1%Engineering bias
-4.1%Marketing bias
10.2%Company MAPE

Company bias is near zero because two large errors cancel: Engineering underforecasts, Marketing overforecasts.

FixApply department-level bias correction and report forecast accuracy by owner each month.

2. Budget vs forecast independence

0.989Corr(budget, forecast)
6.1%Mean move off budget
71Items forecast missed worse

The forecast is the budget with a small edit, not a re-estimate. It predicted worse than budget in 71 of 124 items.

FixRebuild forecasts from drivers; reject submissions hugging budget with no driver change.

3. Variance direction consistency

$738KGross variance
28%Net / gross
6Always-over combos

72% of variance cancels before the board sees it. Six pairs ran over every month, led by Engineering Cloud at 7 of 7. Baseline error, not overspending.

FixReset FY2025 baselines for chronic combos and report gross alongside net variance.
