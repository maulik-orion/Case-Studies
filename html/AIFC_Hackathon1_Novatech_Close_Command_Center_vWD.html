Scalability

What it takes to run this for real

Nothing here depends on a model that doesn't exist yet. The scoring logic in Part 1 is rules plus a lightweight statistical outlier check, the kind of thing a rules engine or a small classifier handles today.

Three systems integrations, not a rebuild

A shared FX rate table both entities' ERPs read at booking time removes the intercompany mismatches at the source instead of catching them at elimination. A single approval-routing rule, "no JE or accrual posts without an assigned owner," closes the orphaned-approver gap that showed up in four separate tabs. And a tolerance-band matching rule in the bank feed handles timing and FX-rounding differences the way a human reconciler already does, just without the wait. None of these require new infrastructure, they require configuring the SAP S/4HANA and Blackline instances already in place.
