Controllable vs. non-controllable factors

What management can act on this quarter, and what it can only monitor or disclose.

Controllable

Inside management's decision rights within the current operating period.

Manufacturing unit cost

Conversion efficiency, yield, scrap, overhead absorption and procurement terms. This is the single largest lever on the page and sits entirely with operations and procurement.

Operating expense spend

Discretionary spend decisions. Landed marginally under the flexed plan, which confirms spend discipline held. No corrective action indicated.

Pricing and discountingPartially controllable

Realized price is negotiated inside a competitive envelope. Price is the fastest available offset to a cost overrun, but it is not unilateral and it carries volume risk.

Non-controllable or structural

Outside management's short-run control, or an artifact of measurement rather than performance.

FX translationNon-controllable

Local currency results are translated at externally sourced January rates. Rate movement is a market outcome, not a management decision, and is not hedged in this data set.

Revenue flex basisStructural, $0 by design

The Restated Budget sets budget revenue equal to actual revenue in every cell. Revenue variance is therefore zero by construction. It is a measurement choice, not a commercial result, and it should not be read as revenue performance.

Country and business line COGS splitEstimated

Source COGS carries no country or business line field. Any split below the company total is a Budget-mix allocation, so geographic COGS and margin readings are illustrative rather than a performance signal.

Notes, assumptions and data preparation

Key judgements behind every figure on the preceding slides. Not exhaustive.

Data preparation and clean-up

Actuals rebuilt from base filesNot taken from the GL summary
Rebuilt from transaction-level source files (Sales by SKU, COGS Manufacturing, Operating Expenses), not the GL summary. These are the reporting basis throughout.
FX ratesExternal source
Local currency translated using January 2026 rates from an outside source, applied consistently to actuals and the restatement. No variance here is a rate-basis difference.
Duplicate and anomaly handlingFlagged, not silently dropped
Duplicate keys consolidated: COGS to rows, Opex to rows. Negative amounts and outliers were flagged and retained, not removed.
Tie-outWhere the numbers agree
Country revenue ties to source to the cent. Company COGS and Opex tie to their source file totals.

Budget restatement basis

RevenueFixed to actual
Budget revenue set equal to actual revenue in every cell, isolating the comparison to cost. Revenue variance is therefore exactly zero by construction, not evidence the commercial plan was met.
Cost of goods soldTreated as variable
Flexed cell by cell: Restated COGS = Budget COGS × (Actual revenue / Budget revenue). The flex absorbs volume and mix, so the residual COGS variance is entirely a unit cost effect.
Operating expenseReduced 80% for comparability
Budget Opex reduced 80% (20% retained per cell) to match one month of spend, treated as fixed. A comparability adjustment only, not an approved cut.
Period scopeJanuary only
Only the 120 January cells were restated. February through December carry the original budget.

Allocation and measurement limitations

COGS below company totalAllocated, not observed
COGS carries plant and SKU, not country or business line. Country and business line COGS is the one real total split by the Budget's own mix, illustrative rather than observed. Plant unit cost is the real basis for accountability.
Business line revenue splitAlso mix-based
Sales carries country, channel and SKU, not business line. Country revenue is real; the business line split follows the Budget mix.
Revenue and COGS SKU coverageNear but not complete
SKU-016 was produced but never sold. SKU-036 was sold but never produced. Coverage between the two files is not identical.
Materiality±2% or ±$1M
Applied at the P&L line level, not by slice. A line is material if either threshold is breached; immaterial lines are still shown in full.
Filters on this packWhat moves and what does not
Country and business line filters recompute the tiles, P&L, bridge and slices. Plant is company-wide and channel responds to country only; neither responds to the business line filter, since it is not in those source files.
